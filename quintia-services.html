What we do

We make startups and small businesses cited by ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. The work covers four pillars: a technically readable site (schema, llms.txt, owned knowledge graph), content shaped for AI extraction (comparison pages, FAQ libraries, glossary), citations from the surfaces LLMs actually read (Reddit, G2, YouTube, category-specific publications), and consistent prompt-level measurement. Our retainers are productized: fixed scope, fixed monthly fee, predictable monthly deliverables.

How our offer is different

Three things we do that most AEO providers don't

We name community and UGC work as headline deliverables, not footnotes

Most agencies monitor citations. Very few earn them. In our April 2026 scan of 19 AEO and GEO providers, only three named Reddit, Quora, or community work as an explicit deliverable. Quora, YouTube, and review-platform seeding were vanishingly rare as named services across the entire field.

Our retainers include these as core scope — Reddit and Quora posting calendars, G2/Capterra review activation, YouTube script production — not as add-ons or aspirational mentions.

We build owned knowledge graphs, not Wikidata workarounds

Most AEO offers stop at schema markup. We build a structured, machine-readable About / Team / Products / FAQ / Glossary surface on your own domain, all linked via JSON-LD. You own the entity layer. We maintain it. This is a meaningful AEO advantage that almost no competitor in the productized lane explicitly sells.

We are honest about what fits in a retainer and what doesn't

Most $2,000-per-month retainers promise "implementation" and cannot deliver it inside the monthly fee. The result is churn at the 90-day mark when clients realize the work did not happen.

Our retainers cover audit, specification, content production, and off-site citation work. Schema deployment, page rewrites, and owned knowledge graph buildout are scoped one-time projects priced on top. This protects margin and protects you from the bait-and-switch that creates churn.

FAQ

Common questions about our services

Five questions we hear most often from founders and marketing leaders evaluating our retainer options.

How is this different from a typical SEO retainer?

SEO retainers focus on ranking pages in Google's blue-link results. Our retainers focus on being cited in AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. The technical foundation overlaps. The off-site work and the content shape are different.

Can I switch tiers mid-engagement?

Yes. Most clients start at Foundation or Momentum and move up after 90 days as they see what is working. We do not lock tier assignments in the SOW.

Do you white-label?

We white-label for a small number of agency partners. Email us if you are an agency considering adding AEO to your offer.

How do you measure success?

Three metrics, reported monthly: prompt-level visibility (share of voice on a tracked prompt set), branded search volume (rising branded search is the leading indicator that AEO is working), and source attribution on inbound leads. As we do not have CRM access, we ask that our clients report the third number to us directly.

Do you guarantee results?

No, and we are skeptical of any AEO agency that does. AI search measurement is too new for hard guarantees. We commit to measurable monthly deliverables and prompt-level reporting. Outcomes follow when execution is consistent.
