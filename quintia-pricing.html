How retainers actually work

Retainer scope is audit, spec, content, and off-site work. Deployment is a separate, scoped project.

Our retainers cover audit, specification, content production, and off-site citation work. Deployment of changes to your site — schema markup, llms.txt files, page rewrites, owned knowledge graph buildout — is a scoped one-time project, priced separately and quoted firm in the SOW.

This split is more honest than the alternative. Most $2,000-per-month retainers in this market promise "implementation" but cannot deliver it inside the monthly fee. The result is the work does not happen and the client churns at month three. We separate retainer scope from deployment scope so what we promise monthly is what we actually ship monthly.

Additional terms

Annual prepay, what's not included, and cancellation

Annual prepay option

Citation Engine clients can prepay 12 months for a 10% discount. Foundation and Momentum are month-to-month after the minimum term, with no annual prepay discount available.

Cancellation policy

Foundation requires a 3-month minimum. Momentum and Citation Engine require a 6-month minimum. After the minimum, we are month-to-month with 30 days written notice. We do not lock clients in beyond the stated minimum.

What is not included in any tier

  • × Press relations, journalist pitching, HARO, or media coverage — we refer to PR partners
  • × Custom-scope strategic engagements above $3,000 per month — we refer to full-service partners
  • × Guaranteed rankings, traffic, or pipeline numbers — AEO measurement is too new for hard guarantees
  • × Account creation, paid review schemes, or anything that violates platform terms

FAQ

Common questions about pricing

Five questions we hear most often when founders and marketing leaders are evaluating the retainer options.

What if I need more content than Citation Engine includes?

Talk to us. We understand some clients have aggressive goals. We can custom scope additional content volume on top of the Citation Engine retainer.

Can I pay quarterly or annually instead of monthly?

Yes. Quarterly billing has no discount but reduces administrative friction. Annual prepay on Citation Engine is 10% off. Foundation and Momentum are billed monthly only.

What is the cancellation policy?

Foundation requires a 3-month minimum. Momentum and Citation Engine require a 6-month minimum. After the minimum, we are month-to-month with 30 days written notice. We do not lock clients in beyond the stated minimum.

Do you offer one-off projects?

The $500 Quick-Start Blueprint is the only one-off productized offer. Deployment add-ons are also priced as one-time projects, but they are scoped against an active retainer's audit and spec — not as standalone projects.

How quickly can we start?

Quick-Start Blueprint kicks off within two business days of intake. Retainers start the month following signature — we do onboarding in the last week of the prior month so month one runs at full cadence from day one.
