Refund & Cancellation Policy

Quick-Start Blueprint · Last updated [DATE]

The short version

The $500 Quick-Start Blueprint is non-refundable. Work begins immediately on your automated diagnostic, and full-Blueprint work begins within two business days of your intake form — most of the effort is spent up front so your deliverable is ready on the 5–10 business day timeline we promise.

Why

Unlike a subscription you can cancel before the next charge, the Blueprint is a fixed-scope, one-time engagement. By the time you'd typically ask for a refund, the work — the automated scan, and often meaningful progress on the human-written recommendations — has already been done.

The retainer credit

If you sign a retainer agreement with us within 30 days of your Blueprint readout call, the full $500 you paid is credited toward your first month. This is the one way your $500 converts into ongoing value beyond the Blueprint itself.

If something actually goes wrong on our end

This policy is about changing your mind, not about us failing to deliver. If we are unable to deliver your automated report or your full Blueprint at all because of a failure on our side — not because the findings weren't what you hoped — contact us at team@quintiavantage.com. We'll investigate, and where we genuinely didn't deliver what we promised, we'll make it right: a refund or a re-run, at our discretion.

What this policy does not cover

  • Disagreement with the report's findings or score
  • A lower score than expected, or one that changes on a later re-scan
  • Changing your mind after purchase but before any work has started
