What it is

The Quick-Start Blueprint is a paid diagnostic plus a 90-day action plan. We test 20 prompts in ChatGPT and Perplexity to baseline your current AI search visibility, audit your site's technical readiness, identify your top five content priorities, build a ranked list of third-party publications and review platforms worth pursuing, and draft a user-generated content plan covering Reddit, Quora, and YouTube. Every item in the action plan is tagged DIY, partner, or done-for-you. It is not a playbook. Free AEO playbooks are oversupplied. The Blueprint is personalized to your site and your category, not a template.

FAQ

Common questions about the Blueprint

Six questions we hear most often from founders and marketing leaders evaluating whether to buy.

What if I do not want to sign a retainer after?

You keep the Blueprint. The 90-day action list is executable on your own or with another partner. Roughly 40% of Blueprint buyers do not move directly to a retainer — they execute the plan internally or with an existing partner. We expect that. The Blueprint is a complete deliverable, not a sales tool.

Is the Blueprint refundable?

No. The work begins within two business days of the intake form. By design we put significant effort in early so the deliverable is ready on time.

Can I share the Blueprint with my team?

Yes. The Blueprint is delivered as a PDF you can circulate internally. We ask that you not share it externally with other vendors as a competitive intelligence document, but internal distribution is expected.

Why is the Blueprint paid?

Free AEO audits are everywhere. They are typically lead magnets, not real diagnostics. A paid Blueprint signals you are serious enough to invest in execution and lets us put real work into the deliverable. The economics also work for us — free audits at scale are not viable for a productized agency.

How is this different from a free AI visibility check?

SaaS tools like Profound, Otterly, and AthenaHQ offer free AI visibility checks. Those are dashboards. They show you data. The Blueprint takes that data, plus a custom audit, plus competitor analysis, and produces a prioritized action plan. The dashboards are useful inputs. The Blueprint is the strategy led by a real marketer.

What happens after the readout call?

Two paths. Either you execute the plan yourselves (or with another partner) and we are done. Or you sign a retainer and we start onboarding within the last week of the current month so the retainer kicks off at full cadence on the first.
