Full AEO explainer

Answer engine optimization (AEO) is the practice of making your business cited by AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews when buyers ask category questions. It overlaps with SEO but rewards different signals: structured data, user-generated content on Reddit and review platforms, and consistent entity information across the web.

SEO optimizes for ranking links in Google's blue-link results. AEO optimizes for being cited inside an AI-generated answer. The technical foundation overlaps — schema, content quality, site speed. The off-site signals do not. SEO rewards backlinks and domain authority. AEO rewards Reddit threads, review sites, structured first-party data, and YouTube transcripts.
