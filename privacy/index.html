Privacy Policy

Quick-Start Blueprint · Last updated [DATE]

1. Who we are

This policy covers Quint·IA Vantage ("we," "us") and the data we collect through blueprint.quintiavantage.com and blueprint.aeolatam.com (the "Site") in connection with the Quick-Start Blueprint service.

2. What we collect

Directly from you, at intake:

  • Company name and domain
  • Competitor names and domains you provide
  • Your contact name and email address
  • Any free-text context you give us about your AI-visibility goals
  • Your preferred language (English/Spanish)

From our payment processor: we never receive or store your card number. Stripe processes payment directly and passes us only confirmation that payment succeeded, a transaction reference, and the billing email you gave Stripe.

Generated by running your scan: the AI engine responses, citation data, and technical findings produced when we test your domain and competitors — this is the content of your report, not separately collected personal data.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

3. How we use it

  • To run your scan and generate your automated report and full Blueprint
  • To email you your deliverables and follow up about your Blueprint readout call
  • To store you as a business lead in our CRM so our team can follow up about next steps
  • In anonymized, aggregated form — with all company-identifying information removed — to describe category-level trends in our own marketing and research, once enough companies in a category exist that no individual company is identifiable

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Your rights

Regardless of where you're located, you can ask us at any time to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your personal information, subject to what we need to retain for legitimate business or legal purposes (e.g., payment records)
  • Opt out of any further marketing contact from us

To exercise any of these, email team@quintiavantage.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Contact and follow-up

After your Blueprint is delivered, we may follow up once about next steps (e.g., a retainer conversation). We do not send ongoing marketing email beyond that unless you opt in separately. Every email we send includes a working unsubscribe or opt-out method.
