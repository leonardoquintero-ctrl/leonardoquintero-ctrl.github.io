## How do US buyers find nearshore software partners now?

More of them start in an AI chatbot. G2's April 2026 research found 51% of B2B software buyers begin research in an AI chatbot more often than in Google, up from 29% a year earlier [S1]. ChatGPT leads that usage at 63% [S3]. A nearshore shortlist often exists before a buyer visits any agency website.

## What is answer engine optimization for a nearshore agency?

Answer engine optimization (AEO) is the work of making your company the source that ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overviews cite when a buyer asks a category question. For a nearshore agency, that question sounds like "Which Colombian firm can staff a React team for a US fintech?"

## Why do AI engines leave out most LatAm nearshore agencies?

They cite what they can verify. An engine needs your company name, services, location and proof to match across your English site, your schema markup, and third-party profiles like Clutch, G2 and LinkedIn. Many LatAm agencies publish Spanish-first pages, inconsistent names, or thin directory profiles, so the engine picks a better-documented competitor.

## Does ranking on Google still bring US leads?

It brings fewer clicks when an AI summary appears. In a Pew Research Center study of 900 US adults, users clicked a traditional result on 8% of visits with an AI summary, versus 15% without one [S5]. They clicked a source inside the summary on 1% of visits [S6]. Keep your rankings, and start tracking whether you are cited inside the answer.

## Can an AI answer change which vendor a US buyer hires?

Yes, according to G2's buyer data. In its April 2026 research, 69% of buyers said they chose a different vendor than they first planned based on AI chatbot guidance [S4]. For a nearshore agency, a missing citation can remove you from a deal you never knew existed.

## What should a nearshore agency fix first?

Fix entity consistency first. Use one exact company name everywhere, publish English service pages that state location, stack, time-zone overlap and pricing model, and add Organization and Service schema. Then complete your Clutch, G2 and LinkedIn profiles with the same facts. Engines cross-check these sources before they recommend a vendor.

## How do you measure whether AI engines recommend your agency?

Track a fixed set of buyer prompts every month. Write 20 to 30 questions a US buyer would ask, run them in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overviews, and log whether you are named, cited, or absent. Compare against three named competitors. Rising branded search and "found you on ChatGPT" leads confirm the trend.
