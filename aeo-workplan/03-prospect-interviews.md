# Prospect interview guide: search language (3–4 calls, 20 min)

Who: LatAm-founded B2B founders/marketing leads who fit the Sales Guide ICP (MX/CO/CL first). Mix of 2 SaaS and 2 nearshore/services firms.

1. When you last looked for an outside marketing or growth vendor, where did you start? (Google, ChatGPT, referrals, LinkedIn, Clutch)
2. Type the exact question you would ask ChatGPT to find help getting US clients. (Record it verbatim. This is the prompt set.)
3. What words do you use for this problem? Probe: "AI SEO", "GEO", "AEO", "aparecer en ChatGPT", "posicionamiento en IA", "lead gen US".
4. Would you search in Spanish or English? Does that change for a US-facing problem?
5. Have your US buyers ever mentioned finding you through ChatGPT/Perplexity?
6. What would make you trust an agency you found through an AI answer?
7. What would you expect a $500 diagnostic to include?

Log answers in a table: prospect, verbatim phrases, language, channel. After 3–4 calls, add the verbatim prompts to the IVIA tracked prompt set and rename pillar H2s to match the exact phrasing.
