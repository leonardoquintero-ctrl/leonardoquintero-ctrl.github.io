## Monthly IVIA re-run
Run IVIA on quintiavantage.com on the 1st of each month with the **same** prompt set (add the interview phrases once). Record the score and every engine/prompt/citation row. Don't change prompts mid-test, or the months won't be comparable. This can be scheduled as a monthly routine once the checker is reachable from the environment.
